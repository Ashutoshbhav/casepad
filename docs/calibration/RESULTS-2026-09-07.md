## Verdict

The tracer clears the **"show scores"** bar (QWK ≥ 0.60). It does **not** clear
**"drive assignment"** (≥ 0.70).

**Recommendation:** keep the Twin **directional** (bands + "early read" — which
is what `SkillProfileCard` already does) until:
1. Ash spot-checks ~5 of the gold labels (single-rater QWK is soft evidence).
2. The sample grows and diversifies (13 sessions, 8 of them estimation, is thin).

Do NOT let the Twin auto-assign the next case off skill state yet.

## Where the tracer disagrees with the human (fix targets)

From per-skill QWK:
- **`arithmetic_accuracy` (QWK −0.15)** — the tracer is HARSHER than the human:
  it scored 0.1–0.2 where the human gave 0.35–0.45 for "mostly right, one
  slip". Prompt fix: tell the tracer to give partial credit for arithmetic
  that is directionally correct with isolated errors, reserve ≤0.15 for
  pervasively broken math.
- **`signposting` (−0.5)** and **`structured_verbalization` (0.0)** — noisy,
  low agreement, but also low-variance (everyone scored ~0.1–0.35), so QWK is
  unstable here. Needs more spread in the sample before it means much.

Strong agreement: `estimation_setup` (0.78), `assumption_transparency` (0.70),
`clarifying_questions` (0.59).

## GEPA run + promotion attempt (later, 2026-09-07)

`optimize-tracer-prompt.ts --n 5` reported: baseline **0.605**, best proposal
**0.688** (Δ +0.083) — sharper wording for `tailored_structure`,
`driver_based_thinking`, `arithmetic_accuracy`, `poise_under_pressure` + a
conservative-scoring rule.

**Promotion FAILED confirmation.** Hand-applied the overrides, re-ran
`calibrate-tracer.ts` → **QWK 0.492** (below the 0.605 baseline). Reverted, not
committed.

**Why:** the optimizer scored each candidate on ONE stochastic tracer run
against a 13-session / ~43-pair gold set. Run-to-run QWK on this set has ranged
0.49–0.69. A single-run score is inside the noise band — "0.688" was an
optimistic draw, not a real gain.

**Fix before trusting the optimizer:** (a) grow the gold set past ~30 sessions;
(b) have `optimize-tracer-prompt.ts` score each candidate as the mean of 2–3
runs (or pin one provider/temperature) so the metric is stable; (c) require the
gain to survive an independent confirmation run before it's eligible to promote.
The proposal file stays as a record.

## Gold set grown to 24 (DB ceiling), 2026-09-07

Scored 11 more sessions (6 completed short case sessions + 5 abandoned/in-progress
genuine case attempts). Rejected 6 (behavioural rounds + corrupted sessions with
garbled non-answers). **The DB has no more gradeable case transcripts** — 24 is
the ceiling until real cohort usage accumulates.

24 sessions / **69 pairs**. Overall QWK **0.483** — LOWER than the 13-session
0.60–0.68.

**Why it went down, and why that's still progress:**
- Most new sessions are short/weak (candidates typed "what", barely engaged), so
  gold + tracer both cluster near the floor. QWK needs score spread; with
  everyone at 0.1–0.3, small disagreements dominate the statistic.
- The earlier 0.60–0.68 was partly a small-n lucky draw (13 sessions, mostly the
  more-engaged ones).

**Per-skill (where there's enough spread to mean something):**
- **Solid:** signposting 0.82, tailored_structure 0.77, mece_decomposition 0.75,
  sanity_checking 0.67, clarifying_questions 0.62.
- **Broken:** `arithmetic_accuracy` **−0.17** (tracer consistently 0.1–0.2 where
  the human gives 0.35–0.5 — harsh, direction confirmed across both passes) and
  `assumption_transparency` **−0.15**.

**Concrete next step (not a full GEPA run):** hand-tune the `observable` for
`arithmetic_accuracy` to grant partial credit for directionally-correct math with
isolated slips, and for `assumption_transparency` sharpen "flags which assumptions
are load-bearing". Re-run `calibrate-tracer.ts`. The Twin stays **directional**
(bands, no hard scores) regardless — the structure/communication skills it's good
at are enough to say "your weakest areas" honestly.

in `taxonomy.ts` — both now spell out the partial-credit band explicitly
